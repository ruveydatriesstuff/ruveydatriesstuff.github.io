Hi! I am a PhD student in Linguistics at the University of Göttingen, where I work on polarity items as part of Pillar II of the ERC project UNPAG. My research areas are semantics, pragmatics and syntax.

I received my master's and bachelor's degrees in Linguistics at Boğaziçi University. For my master's thesis, I worked on the syntax, semantics and pragmatics of Turkish alternative questions under the advisory of Dr. Elena Guerzoni.

For reasons unknown to me, my thesis is not available on the YÖKTEZ database. Please e-mail me for a copy!
